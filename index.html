Chien-Hung Chen (Francis Chen) has been Professor in the Department of Electronic Engineering, College of Intelligent Science and Technology, at I-Shou University since February 2026, and is also Jointly Appointed Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering. He previously served in the Department of Avionics Engineering at the ROC Air Force Academy from February 2010 to February 2026 as Lecturer, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, and Professor, and as Department Chair from July 2019 to June 2025. He was a visiting scholar in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Florida from May to September 2014, and received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology. 陳建宏（Francis Chen）自 2026 年 2 月起擔任義守大學智慧科技學院電子工程學系教授，亦為資訊工程學系合聘教授。他於 2010 年 2 月至 2026 年 2 月任職於空軍軍官學校航空電子工程學系，歷任講師、助理教授、副教授、教授，並於 2019 年 7 月至 2025 年 6 月擔任系主任；亦曾於 2014 年 5 月至 9 月赴美國佛羅里達大學電機電腦系擔任訪問學者。他於國立高雄科技大學取得電機工程博士學位。

He is an IEEE Senior Member and Chair of the IEEE AP-S Tainan Chapter (2025/01–2026/12). In 2026, teams he advised were recognized with the Best Paper Award and the Creativity Award at the 2026 Aegis Cup International Invitational & Defense AI Competition. 他是 IEEE 資深會員，並擔任 IEEE AP-S 臺南分會主席（2025/01–2026/12）。2026 年，他指導的隊伍於2026 神盾盃國際邀請賽暨國防 AI 競賽獲得最佳論文獎與創意獎。

His research interests are in antenna and array design, radar systems and target detection, and cyber-electromagnetic integration. He develops and measures antennas and radar sensing systems, and teaches students how to take an engineering idea from simulation to experiment. 他的研究興趣為天線與陣列設計、雷達系統與目標偵測，以及資安與電磁防護整合。他設計並量測天線與雷達感測系統，也教學生把一個工程構想從模擬做到實驗。
