Chien-Hung Chen (Francis Chen) began active service in 2004 and retired from the ROC Air Force as a Colonel in February 2026, concluding a 31.5-year military career including academy years. In the same month, he joined I-Shou University as Professor in the Department of Electronic Engineering and Joint Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering. During his military service, he received multiple national medals for technology and meritorious service, took part in major national defense R&D programs, and received several overseas honorary commemorative medals. He previously served in the Department of Avionics Engineering at the ROC Air Force Academy from February 2010 to February 2026 as Lecturer, Assistant Professor, Associate Professor, and Professor, and as Department Chair from July 2019 to June 2025. He was a visiting scholar in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Florida from May to September 2014, and received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology. 陳建宏（Francis Chen）於 2004 年開始服役，2026 年 2 月以中華民國空軍上校退役，結束包含軍校在內的 31.5 年軍旅生涯，轉任義守大學電子工程學系教授，亦為資訊工程學系合聘教授。軍旅期間多次獲頒科技類、功績類國家勳獎章，參與國防重大研發案，並曾獲頒多項海外榮譽紀念獎章。他於 2010 年 2 月至 2026 年 2 月任職於空軍軍官學校航空電子工程學系，歷任講師、助理教授、副教授、教授，並於 2019 年 7 月至 2025 年 6 月擔任系主任；亦曾於 2014 年 5 月至 9 月赴美國佛羅里達大學電機電腦系擔任訪問學者。他於國立高雄科技大學取得電機工程博士學位。

He is an IEEE Senior Member and Chair of the IEEE AP-S Tainan Chapter (2025/01–2026/12). In 2026, teams he advised were recognized with the Best Paper Award and the Creativity Award at the 2026 Aegis Cup International Invitational & Defense AI Competition. 他是 IEEE 資深會員，並擔任 IEEE AP-S 臺南分會主席（2025/01–2026/12）。2026 年，他指導的隊伍於2026 神盾盃國際邀請賽暨國防 AI 競賽獲得最佳論文獎與創意獎。

Electromagnetics (I)電磁學(一)

  • Gaussian-surface matching高斯面配對 Match charge distributions with a Gaussian surface that fits the symmetry, then check the pairing.將電荷分佈與適用的高斯面配對（點、線、面、球體），並檢查答案。
  • Magnetic fields in 3D磁場 3D 模擬 A 3D view of the magnetic field of a long straight wire, a loop, and a solenoid, with adjustable current.長直導線、圓形線圈與螺線管等磁場的立體場景，可調電流並顯示磁通密度。
  • Maxwell’s equations in 3DMaxwell 方程式 3D 模擬 Faraday induction, displacement current, electromagnetic-wave propagation, and a solenoid, with adjustable frequency.法拉第感應、位移電流、電磁波傳播與螺線管四種模擬，可調頻率與動畫速度。
